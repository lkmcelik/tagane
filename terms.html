Effective date: 23 November 2026

Tagane is licensed to you under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA). This page adds a short summary of the subscription terms and a health notice. Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

Tagane is for adults aged 18 and older.

2. Subscription

  • Tagane needs a subscription. There is no free version. The subscription, Tagane Pro, comes as a monthly or a yearly plan.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly subscription starts and you are charged. The monthly plan has no trial.
  • Price and payment: the app shows the price in your currency before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends.
  • Automatic renewal: the subscription renews automatically for the same period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your Apple Account is charged for the renewal within the 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel: in your iPhone's Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. In Tagane: Profile › Settings › Subscription › Manage. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Family Sharing isn't available. A subscription belongs to one Tagane account.
  • If your subscription ends, your data stays in your account and syncs again when you subscribe again.
  • Deleting your Tagane account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it as described above.

3. Health notice

Tagane doesn't give medical advice. It is not a medical device and doesn't replace a doctor, a physiotherapist or a coach.

  • If you have an injury or a medical condition, talk to a doctor before you start training with Tagane.
  • Stop exercising and get help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath.
  • BMI, body fat and other metrics are estimates. The goal-body comparison is illustrative only.
  • Choose loads and progressions that are safe for you, and listen to your body.
