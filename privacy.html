Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

In short

  • We collect what Tagane needs to build your plan and keep your training in sync. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across apps and websites.
  • Injuries and limitations, and weights copied from Apple Health, are stored only with your explicit consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Your account data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). You can export it or delete your account in the app.

1. Who we are

Tagane is made by Lokman Çelik, an independent developer. He is the data controller (the "veri sorumlusu" under Türkiye's KVKK) for the personal data described here. Contact: lkmcelik@hotmail.com.

This policy covers the Tagane apps for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, and this website.

2. What we collect and why

DataWhat it includesWhy we use it

With Sign in with Apple, Tagane asks only for your email address, which can be a private relay address. 3. Apple Health

Tagane connects to Apple Health only with the permission you give when iOS asks. Tagane can:

  • On iPhone: save your finished workouts, read and save your body weight, and read active energy.
  • On Apple Watch: save a workout the watch recorded, and read heart rate and active energy during a workout to show them.

Heart rate, active energy and workout statistics never leave your devices. Tagane doesn't store your heart rate.

Weights from Apple Health are copied to your Tagane account only if you agree to it separately in Settings › Apple Health. If you disconnect, Tagane deletes the copied weights from your device and from our servers.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing and is never sold or shared with third parties.

What Tagane saved to Apple Health stays in the Health app after you sign out or delete your account. You can delete it in the Health app.

4. Notifications

Rest-timer alerts and the reminders you turn on are scheduled on your device. We don't use a push service, and we don't store a notification token.

5. Legal bases

Under the EU and UK GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK, we process your data on these grounds:

  • To provide the service you signed up for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)): your account, profile, preferences, training data and subscription.
  • Your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a); KVKK Art. 6): injuries and limitations, and weights copied from Apple Health. You can withdraw it at any time in the app. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before.
  • Our legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)): security logs and request limits, aggregate statistics, diagnostics, and records that stop a refunded subscription from being reused.
  • Legal obligations (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c); KVKK Art. 5(2)(ç)): when the law requires us to keep or disclose data.

7. No ads, no tracking

Tagane has no advertising and adds no third-party analytics, attribution or crash-reporting tools. Only if you choose Google sign-in, Google's sign-in component may collect usage data for Google's own analytics (see the Google Sign-In row above). Tagane doesn't use your device's advertising identifier and doesn't track you across apps and websites. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

8. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile and training data: until you delete your account.
  • Injuries and limitations: until you withdraw your consent or delete your account. When you withdraw it, we also clear your earlier plans of what could hint at your limitations: their exercises, their estimated session lengths and the random value used to create them. Tagane then replaces your current plan with one made without your limitations and clears the replaced plan the same way. The schedule of earlier plans (days, session types and completed sessions) and your workout history stay.
  • Weights copied from Apple Health: until you disconnect Apple Health or delete your account.
  • Consent records: as long as your account exists.
  • App Store notification log: 90 days.
  • Subscription state after you delete your account: the last state of each of your subscriptions (Apple's transaction identifiers, the product, the status and its dates) is kept without your account id or any token for 90 days; a refunded or revoked subscription until 90 days after its access ended. This stops a refund from being undone with an old purchase record.
  • Request counters: hourly; deleted with your account at the latest.
  • Backend logs: our hosting provider, Supabase, keeps the request, database and sign-in logs of our backend (such as IP address, time and the account involved) for 1 day.
  • Sign-in code emails: Gmail keeps a copy of each sent email (your email address and the code) in our sender account. We delete these copies automatically within 7 days, also after you delete your account.
  • Sign-in sessions: our sign-in service keeps the IP address and the device type of each active sign-in session until you sign out on that device or delete your account. We use them only to keep your account secure.
  • Diagnostics: 90 days. The reports are stored without your account id.
  • Backups: our current hosting plan doesn't include database backups, so we keep no backup copies of your data.
  • On your devices: until you sign out or delete the app. A data export file is deleted from your device when you finish sharing it.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile › Settings › Delete account. If you haven't subscribed, use the Account button on the subscription screen. This deletes your data from our servers and from your device, and for Sign in with Apple it revokes Tagane's access to your Apple Account. It doesn't cancel your App Store subscription. What is deleted and what stays.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable format and withdraw your consent. Under KVKK Article 11 you can also ask whether your data is processed, for what purpose, and to whom it is transferred, and claim compensation for unlawful processing.

  • Export: Profile › Settings › Privacy & data › Export my data gives you a copy of your account as one JSON file.
  • Correct: edit your data in Profile.
  • Withdraw consent: Profile › Settings › Privacy & data › Health-data consent, or Apple Health › Disconnect.
  • Delete: see section 9.

For anything else, write to lkmcelik@hotmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the EU, the one where you live or work; in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Every account can read and change only its own data, and the keys that can reach all data exist only on our servers, never in the app. On your device, Tagane uses iOS data protection.

12. Adults only

Tagane is for adults aged 18 and older. Setup accepts adult birth years only. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
